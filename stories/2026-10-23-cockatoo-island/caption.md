This Sydney Harbour island was a convict prison ⛓️
Part 4 of 5: Hidden islands of Australia 🇦🇺

Cockatoo Island is the largest island in Sydney Harbour. A penal establishment opened here in 1839, and convicts quarried the sandstone, built the prison barracks and carved out the Fitzroy Dock by hand.

From 1870 the island became a major shipbuilding yard, and it stayed one for more than a century until the dockyard was vacated in 1992. Quarrying and reclamation grew the island from about 12.9 to 17.9 hectares along the way. 🚢

In 2010 it was World Heritage listed as one of 11 Australian convict sites.

The twist: you can camp here. The island has been open to the public since 2005, with campsites and stays a short ferry ride from the city. ⛺
