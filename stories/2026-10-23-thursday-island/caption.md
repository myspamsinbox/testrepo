Part 5 of 5: Hidden islands of Australia 🇦🇺

Thursday Island sits in the Torres Strait, off the tip of Cape York. Its Torres Strait Islander name is Waiben, often said to mean "no water", because the island had no permanent water supply. The Kaurareg people are its Traditional Owners.

From the late 1800s it boomed as a pearling port. Its boats once employed more than 2,000 men, and about a third of the divers were Japanese. Hundreds of them are buried in the island's Japanese cemetery. 🕊️

In 1890 the RMS Quetta sank in the Torres Strait, and a church on the island, once called the Quetta Memorial Cathedral, still honours those lost.

The twist: today this little island is often called the capital of the Torres Strait. 🌅
